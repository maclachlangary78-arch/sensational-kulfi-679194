LureRater Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

LureRater is an offshore trolling logbook. It stores your lures, strikes, conditions and optional photos against a random device identifier rather than your name or email.

Optional community statistics

If you turn on “Share anonymous lure-performance data”, records you choose to share may be used in aggregate statistics by fish species, lure, size, position and broad location. Sharing is optional and can be turned off for future records. Exact GPS coordinates and device identifiers are not displayed in community results. Results require a minimum number of records.

Location and photos

Location is read only while you use the app and may be stored with a strike if you grant permission. Photos are uploaded only when you attach them. Sea-temperature lookups send your current coordinates to Open-Meteo.

Deletion

You can delete individual strikes in the app. To request deletion of data associated with your device, use the support contact on the App Store page.

Third parties and children

The app is hosted on Netlify, uses Open-Meteo for marine weather, has no advertising SDK, and is not directed at children under 13.
