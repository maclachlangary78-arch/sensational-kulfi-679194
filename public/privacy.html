LureRater Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

LureRater is an offshore trolling logbook. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

No accounts

LureRater does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any login. When you first open the app it creates a random identifier on your device. That identifier is used only to keep your logbook separate from everyone else's. It is not linked to your identity.

What we store

  • Your logbook — lures, sizes, hook rigs, spread positions and the strikes you log, stored on our servers against your device's random identifier.
  • Location — when you log a strike, the app records your GPS position with it, if you have granted location permission. Location is only read while you are using the app.
  • Photos — any photo you choose to attach to a strike is resized on your device and uploaded with that strike.
  • Settings and the day's spread — kept on your device only.

Third parties

To fill in sea surface temperature, the app sends your current coordinates to the Open-Meteo marine weather service. Nothing else is sent with that request. The app is hosted on Netlify. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual strikes from within the app. Deleting the app removes its identifier from your device; data stored against that identifier is then no longer reachable by anyone. To request removal of all data associated with your device, contact us using the support link on the LureRater App Store page.

Children

LureRater is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
